## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-precision Swiss modernist utility fused with ultra-refined dark-mode product engineering. Inspired by contemporary architectural productivity tools, it avoids clinical command-line density in favor of tactile spaciousness, quiet confidence, and immediate consumer readability. 

The aesthetic is constructed on a pure pitch-black foundation layered with crystalline translucent tiers, razor-sharp 1px border geometry, and precise optical micro-interactions. Information density is treated with editorial restraint: spacious item listings, unhurried negative space, and typographic prominence given to curated metadata, marketplace sources, and standardized Swiss currency values. 

The emotional response should feel like handling a bespoke mechanical timepiece: zero ornamentation for the sake of decoration, perfect micro-radius balance, instantaneous clarity, and frictionless spatial hierarchy across Swiss secondary marketplace aggregations.

## Colors

The palette establishes an obsidian continuum, contrasting pure black with precisely calibrated zinc steps and subtle glowing source markers.

### Core Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#000000`)**: Deep pitch black reserved for primary view background, creating an infinite void effect.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#09090B` / 80% opacity)**: The structural surface layer for navigation bars, filters, and major containers.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#121215` / 90% opacity)**: The primary card and active panel layer.
- **Surface Elevated (`#18181B`)**: Popovers, contextual menus, and focused card states.
- **Border Default (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`)**: Precision 1px micro-border applied globally.
- **Border Hover (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`)**: Illuminated micro-border on interactive hover.

### Typography & Content
- **Text Primary (`#F4F4F6`)**: Near-white for headlines, core prices, and primary labels.
- **Text Secondary (`#A1A1AA`)**: Neutral zinc for body text, item specifications, and secondary timestamps.
- **Text Muted (`#71717A`)**: Passive metadata, table column headers, and structural delimiters.

### Source Identity Accent Radiance
Marketplace provenance is identified via soft luminescence rather than aggressive solid branding:
- **Ricardo**: Deep coral ember (`#FF3B30` core, `rgba(255, 59, 48, 0.12)` ambient fill, `rgba(255, 59, 48, 0.25)` edge trace).
- **Tutti**: Vivid azure cyan (`#007AFF` core, `rgba(0, 122, 255, 0.12)` ambient fill, `rgba(0, 122, 255, 0.25)` edge trace).
- **Anibis**: Emerald lime (`#30D158` core, `rgba(48, 209, 88, 0.12)` ambient fill, `rgba(48, 209, 88, 0.25)` edge trace).
- **Swiss Accent (Tertiary)**: Alpine crimson (`#E35238`) applied sparingly for system-critical alerts or verified Swiss merchant badges.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Geist** throughout all roles to provide geometric purity, neutral clarity, and monoline precision. 

### Currency Representation (CHF)
Swiss Franc listings adhere strictly to standard Swiss typographical convention:
- Currency symbol `CHF` renders using the `code-chf` token in `Text Muted` (`#71717A`) with uppercase tracking (`0.05em`), offset by an unbreaking non-proportional space (e.g., `CHF 1’250.–` or `CHF 89.00`).
- Numerical values use tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure vertical alignment throughout listings, comparisons, and aggregations.
- Thousands separators follow the Swiss typographic apostrophe (`’`), never commas.

### Hierarchy & Letter Spacing
Negative tracking scales tighter as headline scale increases, ensuring titles lock together with poster-like Swiss tension. Body copy maintains neutral tracking to maximize scanning speed across varying description lengths. Labels and micro-badges employ positive tracking to guarantee legibility at compact scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a modular fluid architecture structured on an 8pt spatial grid with generous breathing room. 

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12-column grid within a max-width container of 1600px. Column gutters are pinned to `gutter` (`1.5rem` / 24px) with expansive outer canvas padding of `margin` (`3rem` / 48px). Aggregator card streams flow in a standard 3-column or 4-column matrix.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column layout. Aggregator card streams collapse into a 2-column format. Filter controls migrate from a persistent sticky sidebar to a floating top utility bar.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout. Margin resets to `margin-mobile` (`1.25rem` / 20px) with gutters at `gutter-mobile` (`1rem` / 16px). Cards reflow into a clean, unified single-column stream with horizontal image galleries.

### Spacing Philosophy
Whitespace is deliberate and functional. Instead of packing items into dense tabular rows, elements receive expansive internal card padding (`space-lg`) to balance imagery, price tags, and multi-source comparison points. Margins between disparate content domains (such as search criteria, query aggregations, and individual listings) prioritize clear vertical stratification.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids heavy skeuomorphic drop-shadows. Depth is articulated through translucent obsidian planes, ambient glows, and precision glassmorphic borders.

### The Glassmorphic Layering Matrix
1. **Floor 0 (Absolute Base)**: Solid pitch black (`#000000`). Void ground for canvas scrolling.
2. **Floor 1 (Submerged Structural Layer)**: Sticky headers, persistent filter bars, and backdrop docking menus. Styled with `#09090B` at 70% opacity coupled with a `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an underside hairline border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
3. **Floor 2 (Card & Surface Layer)**: Feed items, detail drawers, and filter modules. Encased in `#121215` with an ultra-thin 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Hover transitions do not elevate on the Z-axis with aggressive drop shadows; instead, the surface border lightens to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.20)` and gains a subtle top specular gradient highlight (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.05) 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0) 100%)`).
4. **Floor 3 (Overlays & Command Dialogs)**: Modals, quick-look previews, and switcher palettes. Surface `#18181B` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, an outer micro-border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, and a diffused ambient occlusion shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.

### Ambient Radiance
Source chips and focal callouts emit localized luminescent blurs (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px var(--source-glow-color)` at 15% opacity), providing functional illumination in total darkness without introducing visual fatigue.

## Shapes

The design system uses a balanced geometric contour (`roundedness: 2`). Radii are strictly proportioned to avoid either sharp Brutalist edges or overly playful bubble geometry, achieving a high-end hardware aesthetic.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Source Badges)**: 8px (`0.5rem`).
- **Cards, Panels, and Containers (`rounded-lg`)**: 16px (`1rem`).
- **Modals, Floating Dialogs, and Command Hubs (`rounded-xl`)**: 24px (`1.5rem`).
- **Pill Attributes**: Fully rounded radii (`9999px`) are strictly reserved for circular icon actions and micro status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Crisp white fill (`#F4F4F6`) with pitch-black text (`#000000`), weight 500. Zero visible shadow at rest; scales down by 0.98 on active click.
- **Secondary Action**: Translucent surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`) with a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`), text `#F4F4F6`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent base with text `#A1A1AA`. On hover, text brightens to `#F4F4F6` with an ultra-subtle tint (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`).

### Source Chips (Ricardo, Tutti, Anibis)
- Inline status capsules showing listing provenance.
- Layout: Height 24px, radius 6px, horizontal padding 8px.
- Visuals: Tinted dark background matching source hue (8% opacity), 1px micro-border (20% opacity of source color), accompanied by a 4px illuminated source dot emitting a faint 6px glow radius. Typography in `label-sm` with upper tracking.

### Cards (Aggregator Marketplace Items)
- Surface: `#121215` with a continuous 1px outline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`). Radius 16px.
- Internal structure: High-ratio aspect preview image (16:10 or 4:3) with smooth rounded inner corners (10px) separated by 12px margins from card borders.
- Header row: Source chip alongside unified Swiss location label (e.g., *Zürich, ZH* · *2h ago*).
- Body: Two-line clamped title in `headline-sm`, high contrast.
- Footer: Dedicated price container displaying CHF notation in `price-card`, contrasting against a subtle secondary compare price or original retail value if available.

### Input & Search Fields
- Global Search Bar: Pill or soft-corner container with `#09090B` surface, height 48px, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`. Inner keyboard shortcut badge (`⌘K`) in muted zinc.
- Standard Form Field: Height 40px, padding 12px, font `body-md`. Focus state shifts the border instantly to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.40)` accompanied by a 1px subtle white glow.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- Checkbox: 18x18px box, radius 4px, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.20)`. Checked state fills `#F4F4F6` with an obsidian SVG checkmark.
- Segmented Filters: Obsidian track (`#09090B`), inset 2px. Active segment features an elevated `#18181B` tile with a fine specular border.

### Multi-Platform Price Comparison Indicator
- An inline analytical badge showing lowest price delta across crawled platforms.
- Pill design, positive differences rendered in muted emerald tint (`#30D158`), neutral in zinc (`#71717A`).
